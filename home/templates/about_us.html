About Us - Perpex Restaurant Management

This is a platform to help organize restaurant data. Keeping all restaurant information in one place and allowing users to manage that data easily.

History

We have seen a lot of restaurants struggle to manage their data which resulted in their business processes suffering. We decided to create a solution to this problem to help restaurants manage data easily.

Mission

Our mission is to help data streamline business processes by helping restaurants manage their data easily to help with customer interactions and supply chain management.
